Undergraduate thesis · accepted September 2025

Efficiency of Eigenvalue-Spread Based Combination Rule for Distributed Blind Equalization in Complex Networks

Supervisor: Dr. Nargis Parvin, Associate Professor, Dept. of CSE, BAIUST

  • ProblemWireless sensor networks need to estimate signals without training sequences, and nodes with noisy channels drag the whole network down.
  • ApproachA new combination rule that gives more weight to nodes with a lower eigenvalue spread, built into the distributed Generalized Sato Algorithm.
  • ResultThe indirect EVS method had the fastest MSE convergence and lowest symbol error rate at every SNR tested, beating the Metropolis, Relative-Degree and Uniform rules.
MATLABGraph theoryWelch method · FFTEigenvalue decomposition4-QAMScale-Free & Small-World networks
